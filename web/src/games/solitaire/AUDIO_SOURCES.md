# Solitaire Audio Sources

All audio assets used in the solitaire game are CC0 (Creative Commons Zero) licensed,
meaning they are in the public domain. No attribution is legally required, but sources
are documented here for traceability.

## Assets

### `card-flip.mp3`

- **Source file:** `contact1.wav` from the "Playing Card Sounds" pack
- **Source URL:** https://opengameart.org/content/playing-card-sounds
- **Author:** Brian MacIntosh (optional attribution, not required by CC0)
- **License:** CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain Dedication)
  https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- **Duration:** ~81ms
- **Description:** Short card contact snap — used when a card flips face-up (stock
  draw to talon, tableau auto-flip after a move).

### `card-move.mp3`

- **Source file:** `playcard.wav` from the "Card Game Sounds" pack
- **Source URL:** https://opengameart.org/content/card-game-sounds
- **Author:** Unknown (no attribution listed on OpenGameArt page)
- **License:** CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain Dedication)
  https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- **Duration:** ~188ms
- **Description:** Card placement sound — used when a card is successfully dropped
  on a tableau column or foundation pile.

### `shuffle.mp3`

- **Source file:** `shuffle.wav` from the "Card Game Sounds" pack
- **Source URL:** https://opengameart.org/content/card-game-sounds
- **Author:** Unknown (no attribution listed on OpenGameArt page)
- **License:** CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain Dedication)
  https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- **Duration:** ~563ms
- **Description:** Riffle/shuffle sound — used when stock is recycled (talon to
  stock on empty stock click) and on new game deal.

### `game-won.mp3`

- **Source file:** `cuckoo.wav` from the "Card Game Sounds" pack
- **Source URL:** https://opengameart.org/content/card-game-sounds
- **Author:** Unknown (no attribution listed on OpenGameArt page)
- **License:** CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain Dedication)
  https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- **Duration:** ~438ms
- **Description:** Bright celebratory chime — played once when the player wins
  (all 52 cards on foundations).
